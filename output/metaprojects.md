Where SECURE Tool built expertise renting and moving physical tools to people who need them, MetaJobs applies the exact same playbook to digital tools; gamepiece NFTs that unlock access to in‑game, income‑generating tasks.</p><p>When the “job” lives inside a game, the “tool” is your gamepiece: the spaceship, the character, the weapon, the access pass. No tool, no work.
