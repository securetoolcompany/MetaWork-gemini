<h2>Monetize Your Work</h2> <h4>SECURE MetaWork empowers artists and creators to monetize their work by providing a secure, transparent platform for global designers to pay artists for using their IP to design a product.</h4> <img width="1024" height="541" src="https://securemetawork.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Submit-IP.png" alt="" /> <h1>Step 1 - Submit IP</h1> <p>The first step is submitting your IP (Intellectual Property) for approval. Give your art a title, name your price, and hit submit. Your IP will be reviewed and you’ll be notified in 48 – 72 hours, by email and MetaWork DM, if your IP is approved.</p><p>We currently accept image files and 3D print files.</p> <a href="https://securemetawork.com/upload-your-ip/"> Submit Your IP Here </a> <img width="1024" height="541" src="https://securemetawork.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/secure-storage.png" alt="" /> <h1>Step 2 - Tokenize</h1> <p>Behind the scenes, our IP Review Team tokenizes your IP and transparently stores it in a cryptographically secure, decentralized digital vault: no one gets in without the key (ie, NFT).</p><p>“Decentralized” means it’s stored all over the world, so you’ll always have access. Tokenization is more complex, but the button below explains everything.</p> <a href="https://securemetawork.com/about-tokenization/"> How Does Tokenization Work? </a> <img width="1024" height="562" src="https://securemetawork.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/save-designs.png" alt="" /> <h1>Step 3 - Sell</h1> <p>Once approved, you’ll receive a link to a page that has all your work displayed.
